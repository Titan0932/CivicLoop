As-We-Heard-It report

Project Status Update November 2023

The design concept has been slightly revised in response to public concerns about the health of existing trees and storm drainage.

  • Sidewalk narrowed to 1.8 m and shifted an additional 0.5 m into the street to reduce risk to trees.
  • Two trees marked for removal with two-for-one replacements planned.
  • On-street parking will not be permitted on either side of the road in the revised segment.
